Quick guide

How to access Walar Movies.

You need Twingate, approval on the Walar network, and your preferred browser. Once approved, the server address stays the same.

1

Install Twingate and create an account

Install the Twingate app from twingate.com on any device you want to use for Walar Movies. Create an account too; it is important for access approval.

Already have the app and an account? You can skip step 1.
2

Request access to the Walar network

Open Twingate and request access to the walar.twingate.com network. After approval, you will be able to connect to Walar Server at 10.0.0.5 .

3

Open Walar Movies in your browser

Go to your preferred browser and enter the server address: 10.0.0.5:8096 . That is where Walar Movies lives.

Connection disclaimer

Your browser may show an unsecured connection warning. For this private access flow, no valuable information is exchanged, so it is not a practical issue. HTTPS is currently being worked on.
